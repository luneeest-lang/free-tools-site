Korean hourly and monthly wage calculator

Convert an hourly wage to a monthly wage under Korean labor rules, or a monthly wage back to an hourly rate. The calculator adds paid weekly holiday hours (주휴수당, juhyu sudang) based on your weekly contractual hours, so it works for part-time and full-time jobs.

The 2027 Korean minimum wage is 10,700 won per hour. For a 40-hour week that is 2,236,300 won per month, according to the Ministry of Employment and Labor.

How to use

  1. Choose hourly → monthly or monthly → hourly.
  2. Enter the hourly or monthly wage in won.
  3. Enter your weekly contractual hours. Five 8-hour days is 40.
  4. Turn off "Include weekly holiday pay" to see the amount without it.

How it works

Weekly holiday hours = weekly contractual hours ÷ 40 × 8, only when weekly hours are 15 or more.

Monthly paid hours = (weekly hours + holiday hours) × 365 ÷ 7 ÷ 12, rounded to a whole hour. For a 40-hour week that is (40 + 8) × 4.345 ≈ 208.6, or 209 hours.

Monthly wage = hourly wage × monthly paid hours. Hourly wage = monthly wage ÷ monthly paid hours, rounded to the nearest won. Weekly holiday pay = hourly wage × holiday hours.

Example

At the 2027 minimum wage of 10,700 won and 40 hours a week, monthly paid hours are 209 and the monthly wage is 10,700 × 209 = 2,236,300 won. Weekly holiday pay is 10,700 × 8 = 85,600 won.

At the same rate for 20 hours a week, holiday hours are 4, monthly paid hours are about 104, and the monthly wage is 1,112,800 won. At 14 hours a week there is no holiday pay and monthly paid hours are about 61.

Good to know

  • Weekly holiday pay requires full attendance on your scheduled working days that week.
  • Whether you reach 15 hours a week is judged on the average over four weeks.
  • Amounts are before income tax and social insurance deductions.
  • Overtime, night, and holiday premiums are not included, and employers may round monthly hours differently.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a 40-hour week 209 hours a month?

Forty working hours plus 8 paid holiday hours is 48 hours a week. Multiplied by the average number of weeks in a month (365 ÷ 7 ÷ 12 ≈ 4.345), that is about 208.6, which rounds to 209. The Ministry of Employment and Labor uses 209 hours for the monthly minimum wage.

Do I get weekly holiday pay if I work under 15 hours?

No. Under the Labor Standards Act, the paid weekly holiday rule does not apply to workers whose contractual hours average fewer than 15 a week over four weeks.

Does it include overtime or night pay?

No. It covers base pay for up to 40 contractual hours a week. Overtime, night, and holiday work premiums are calculated separately.
