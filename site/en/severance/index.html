Frequently asked questions

When is severance pay owed?

Under Korean law it is generally owed after at least one year of continuous service, when you leave the job.

How is the average daily wage calculated?

Total wages for the last three months divided by the number of days in that period.

Why can my actual payment differ?

Bonus treatment, tax deductions, and company rules can all change the final amount.
