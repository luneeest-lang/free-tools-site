How to use

  1. Enter your total wages for the last three months.
  2. Enter the number of days in those three months, usually about 90.
  3. Enter your total days worked, from your start date to your end date.

How it works

The Korean Ministry of Employment and Labor gives this formula:

Severance pay = average daily wage × 30 days × (total days worked ÷ 365)

The average daily wage is your total wages for the three months before you leave, divided by the total number of calendar days in those three months. If that figure is lower than your ordinary wage (통상임금), the ordinary wage is used instead.

Example

Suppose your total wages for the last three months were 9,000,000 and those months had 90 days. Your average daily wage is 9,000,000 ÷ 90 = 100,000. After exactly one year (365 days), severance pay is 100,000 × 30 × (365 ÷ 365) = 3,000,000. After two years (730 days), it is 6,000,000.

Good to know

  • This calculator follows Korean law, the Employee Retirement Benefit Security Act. It does not apply to jobs outside Korea.
  • According to the Ministry, workers with less than one year of continuous service, and workers who are scheduled to work fewer than 15 hours a week on average over four weeks, are excluded.
  • Employers must pay within 14 days of the date the payment becomes due, unless both sides agree to extend the deadline. The right to claim expires if it is not exercised for three years from the day after you leave.
  • The calculator does not compare the result with your ordinary wage. If your ordinary wage is higher than the average wage, your actual severance pay can be higher.
  • Taxes are not included. How bonuses and paid leave allowance are counted depends on your company rules and the Ministry guidance, and if you are enrolled in a company pension plan, payment may work differently.
  • This is general information, not legal advice. For your exact amount, check with the Ministry of Employment and Labor, the Korea Workers' Compensation and Welfare Service, or your employer.

Frequently asked questions

When is severance pay owed?

Under Korean law it is generally owed after at least one year of continuous service, when you leave the job.

How is the average daily wage calculated?

Total wages for the last three months divided by the number of days in that period.

Why can my actual payment differ?

Bonus treatment, tax deductions, and company rules can all change the final amount.
