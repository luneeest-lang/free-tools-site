Frequently asked questions

What is an equal-payment loan?

You pay the same amount each month. Early payments are mostly interest, and the principal share grows over time.

How is a 0% loan calculated?

There is no interest, so the monthly payment is the principal divided by the number of months.

Does this include early repayment fees?

No. Check your lender's terms for any early repayment fee.
