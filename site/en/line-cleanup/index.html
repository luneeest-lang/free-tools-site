Line break and space cleanup

Text copied from PDFs or web pages often has extra spaces at line ends and repeated blank lines. This tool trims spaces at the start and end of each line, reduces repeated blank lines to one, and removes blank lines at the very start and end.

Spaces between words are left unchanged.

Turn on "Remove line breaks and join into one line" to get a single line, with one space where each line break was.

Cleaned result

How to use

  1. Paste the text you want to clean into the input box.
  2. The cleaned text appears in the output box.
  3. Copy the output for use.

Frequently asked questions

Are spaces between words removed?

No. Only spaces at the start and end of each line are removed.

Can I remove all blank lines and line breaks?

Yes. Turn on "Remove line breaks and join into one line" to get a single line. Without it, one blank line is kept to separate paragraphs.

Is my text saved?

No. Everything is processed in your browser.

Values you enter are never stored or sent to a server. Advertising partners may use cookies.
