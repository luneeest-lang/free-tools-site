Remove duplicate lines

Clean up line-based lists such as email addresses, keywords, or contacts. The first occurrence of each line is kept, and later copies are removed.

With case-insensitive matching on, lines that differ only in capitalization count as duplicates. Blank lines are not checked for duplicates.

How to use

  1. Paste your line-based list into the input box.
  2. Turn on case-insensitive matching if you need it.
  3. Check the result and the number of removed lines.

Frequently asked questions

Does the original order change?

No. The first occurrence of each line keeps its position.

Are lines with different spaces duplicates?

No. Lines must match exactly, including spaces, to count as duplicates.

Is my list saved?

No. Everything runs in your browser.

Values you enter never leave your device.
