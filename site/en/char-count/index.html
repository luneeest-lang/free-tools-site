Character counter

Check text that has a length limit, such as a bio, a post, or a message. It shows the character count both with and without spaces.

Words are counted by splitting on spaces, so the number may differ slightly from other word counters for languages without spaces.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your text into the box.
  2. Read the character counts, word count, and line count.
  3. Edit the text and check the numbers again as needed.

Frequently asked questions

Are spaces counted as characters?

Both are shown. Check the count with spaces and the count without spaces.

How are emoji counted?

Counts are per code unit, so one emoji may count as two characters.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser only.

Values you enter never leave your device.
