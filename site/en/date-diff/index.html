Frequently asked questions

Is the start or end date included?

Neither is counted as an extra day. The result is the number of days from the start date to the end date.

Are leap years handled?

Yes. Calculations use the real calendar, including February 29.

How are weeks and days calculated?

Weeks are the total days divided by 7, and the remainder is the leftover days.
